Where is your copy?

Social Saver on a phone is a private copy that only you use: on your own free Vercel address, or on your PC at home. Type its address once and this page opens it from then on.

No copy yet?

  1. Free, no PC: your own Vercel address, in about two minutes.
  2. No limits: your own PC runs it; the phone uses it over Wi-Fi.
  3. Android only: the app, with nothing to host.
How to get your own copy

Keep it on the home screen

iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: in Chrome tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.

The address stays in this browser on this phone. This page sends it nowhere and saves nothing else; there is no shared Social Saver that everyone uses.
